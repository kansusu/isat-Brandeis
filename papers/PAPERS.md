# Papers connected to the RGB perception pipeline

## Included as PDFs

- **Zhu, Y., Dey, I., Lai, K., & Pustejovsky, J. (to appear).** *Nonverbal Behavior Recognition in Multimodal Interactions from RGB Video.* Book chapter. The pipeline, the NICE evaluation (12 clips, accuracy 0.73, F1 0.59), and the error analysis. Source of every figure in `../figures/`.
- **Palmer, D., Zhu, Y., et al. (2025).** *Speech Is Not Enough: Interpreting Nonverbal Behavior in Multiparty Dialogue.* AAAI 2025. Why nonverbal signals must be aligned with speech; the 3D version of gaze from Azure Kinect skeletons (ear-to-nose vector).

## Not included (add the PDFs yourself)

- **TRACE: Real-Time Common Ground Tracking** (NAACL 2025 Demo, co-author). Live multimodal system (FasterWhisper, openSMILE prosody, 5–6 FPS). https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-demo.5.pdf · code: https://github.com/csu-signal/TRACE
- **Multimodal Situational Awareness** (HCII 2025, first author). The deployed classroom pipeline. *Title differs between the CV ("…in Collaborative Learning") and the slides ("…: Neuro-Symbolic AI for Real-Time HCI in the Classroom"); check the published title.*
- **Modeling Theory of Mind in Multimodal HCI** (HCII 2024, first author). The bridge from perception to Theory of Mind used on slide 7.
